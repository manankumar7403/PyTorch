# EXTRA - CURRICULUM
* Watch [MIT's Introduction to Deep Computer Vision] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iaSUYvmCekI&list=PLtBw6njQRU-rwp5__7C0oIVt26ZgjG9NI&index=4) lecture. This will give you a great intuition behind convolutional neural networks.

* Spend 10-minutes clicking through the different options of the [PyTorch Vision library](https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/index.html)

* Lookup "most common convolutional neural networks", what architectures do you find? Are any of them contained within the [`torchvision.models`](https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/models.html)

* For a large number of pretrained PyTorch computer vision models as well as many different extensions to PyTorch's computer vision functionalities check out the [PyTorch Image Models library `timm`](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models) (Torch Image Models) by Ross Wightman.
